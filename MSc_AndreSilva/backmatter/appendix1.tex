\section{Per-Product Hybrid Model Performance}
\label{sec:heatmaps}

The following heatmaps show the percentage relative improvement of each graph-augmented hybrid model over its respective baseline (LSTM or MLP), computed as:

\begin{equation}
    \Delta_{\%} = \frac{\text{Baseline} - \text{Hybrid}}{\text{Baseline}} \times 100
\end{equation}

\noindent A positive value (green) indicates the hybrid achieves lower MAE or higher POCID than the baseline for that product. A negative value (red) indicates the baseline outperforms the hybrid.
